ELI Restaurant

ELI is the warm taste of the North in every dish. We cook with carefully selected local produce: fish, meat, wild mushrooms, berries and aromatic herbs.

The menu features delicate fish soup and creamy soups, salmon, muksun and pike dishes, hearty hot dishes of venison, beef, elk and pork with root vegetable sides, and desserts with lingonberries or cranberries.

A cosy atmosphere, attentive service and special seasonal offers will make your visit especially enjoyable. Come and taste Northern traditions with a modern twist!
